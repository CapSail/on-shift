# Final critique: SHIFTY-1 (v1.0.0 build in `dist/`, 2026-11-13)

Playtest at 390x844 (isMobile, hasTouch, Android UA) against `vite preview` on the current `dist/`. Throwaway Playwright scripts lived outside the project. Covered: install gate, onboarding, first run, a second run the same day, day 2, day 8 at temperature 1.0, the daily eval and share, the 20Q benchmark, red team, speed round (mashed for 60 s), drill, the model card with an exam date ~20 days out, syllabus, about and earbud. About 110 screenshots. **No console errors or page errors anywhere.** No horizontal overflow on any screen.

Items already fixed in `content-qa.md` / `review_design.md` were checked and are not re-reported. These are confirmed fixed: verdict under the dock, epoch rule, onboarding CTA, same-day SRS promotion, earbud colours, speed mashing (17 right − 35 wrong scores 0), the benchmark header, JAILBROKEN overflow and FACT labels.

## 1. Verdict

**B+. Ship-ready after three one-line-to-ten-line fixes (B1–B3 below).** The core loop works and is genuinely good: pretest → epoch → spaced practice, rule-first feedback, and SOC analogies. The jokes land ("Error 418: I am a teapot. Also, wrong.", "Gradient descended. Happily.", the Amazon Vibes line). Accuracy work is thorough. What stops it from shining:

- the screen often opens **scrolled halfway down**, hiding the bar, the card's kind/flag header and sometimes the 🎯 Sure toggle you're supposed to tap first;
- on a missed multi/order/match the **correct answer is scrolled off-screen**, so you learn *that* you were wrong but not *what* was right;
- after any red-team try, the home screen shows a **visibly broken tile**;
- the "exam conditions" benchmark still leaks right/wrong through the combo counter and Shifty's face.

## 3. Polish: 10 highest-impact, cheapest first

1. **Make every screen start at the top** (B1). It's a one-liner, and it's the single biggest "feels janky" fix: the ✕, progress bar, combo and card label are all part of the game's feel.
2. **Show "✔ Answer: …" in every miss panel** (B2). Cheap, and it turns every miss into a learning event. It also makes the SOC BRAIN box land, since it follows the actual answer.
3. **Make the tier-up an end-screen moment instead of a mid-card toast.** Use a full-width "⬆ MODEL UPGRADE: Tokenizer — *knows words exist*" card with Shifty in a new pose, plus a Dale line ("I'm putting 'Tokenizer' on my LinkedIn"). It removes the B7 occlusion and makes the 10-tier ladder feel earned.
4. **"Drill these misses" button** on the benchmark, daily, red-team and speed end screens: `startDrill(misses.map(k=>k.c))`. Every high-stakes mode then feeds straight back into learning. That's a few lines of code and a big gain in effectiveness.
5. **Fix the readiness vs benchmark mismatch on home and the model card.** Right now the tile says ≈147/1000 while the benchmark says 649. Show "≈147 · 9/68 concepts trained" on the tile. On the model card, explain in one line: "Counts untrained concepts as zero. Your benchmark tests what you've seen." Otherwise it reads as a bug or as "I'm failing".
6. **Comeback and streak-loss beat.** On day 8 the chip silently drops to 🔥 0. Add a home bubble when `liveStreak()` resets ("I was offline 6 days. I forgot what a token is. 7 reviews due — let's fix my weights."), and explain the freeze once. That's cheap, funny, and it pulls lapsed players back in.
7. **Better share text.** Add tier and streak plus a rotating Shifty quip ("SHIFTY-1 Daily Eval #12 · 8/10 · Base Model 🔥4 · *'I only hallucinated twice'*"). Use 🟩 for sure-right in the grid so the confidence mechanic shows up socially. Use `navigator.share` with the URL as a separate field.
8. **Red-team juice.** On each correct answer, stamp "BLOCKED 🛡" over Dale's taunt, shake the HP bar, and let Dale react ("he patched it?!"). On a miss, show "INJECTION SUCCEEDED" in red. Also show the per-card attack line *as* the scenario where one fits (it's already on the open list). It's the most game-like mode and currently plays like a quiz with a health bar.
9. **End-of-run recap of rules.** Under "New training data", show each concept's one-line rule, not just "comes back tomorrow". It's a free retrieval cue, and it's what someone screenshots to study on the bus.
10. **Benchmark exam polish.** Hide the combo and Shifty reactions (B4). Add a 1-tap "flag for review" and allow changing an answer before the auto-advance, or replace the 260 ms auto-advance with a "Next" button. Add a pace-colour clock (green under 83 s/Q, amber over). The exam-sim mode then feels like the real thing.

## 4. Things that could mislead a learner

- **Hidden correct answers on misses (B2).** A player who doesn't scroll up leaves a missed order, match or multi card without ever seeing the right answer. They'll meet the concept again, but the specific misconception isn't corrected on the spot.
- **The benchmark leaks correctness (B4).** Scores are inflated if the player reacts to the combo or Shifty's face, and "Book the exam when benchmarks are 750+" relies on this number.
- **Readiness ≈147 next to a 649 benchmark (P5).** Two "/1000" numbers that disagree by 500 points with no explanation. Learners will either distrust both or panic.
- **The temperature 1.0 break-even claim (B9).** "Worth it above 50%" is wrong relative to careful lean. That's minor, but this is the game's one lesson about calibration.
- **"TEMP 1.0" in red team (B5)** makes players think their setting changed.
- **Speed "Fix these" (B6)** pairs "That describes X" with a different term's definition. Read out of context, it can teach the wrong pairing.
- No wrong keys were seen in about 120 cards played. Generated fact cards read cleanly ("What is Token?" is slightly awkward grammar; "What is a token?" would read better).

## Already good (keep it)

The install gate's copy and steps are clear. Onboarding fits above the fold. Pretest framing ("Wrong guesses are allowed. Encouraged, even. I am a role model.") is excellent. Rule-first feedback with the SOC BRAIN box only on a miss is the right call. The proper-scoring Sure toggle with a hint is in place. Speed round mashing now scores 0. Earbud colours and copy are correct. The syllabus modal ("Locked? Nothing is locked: read ahead any time") is good. Dale's lines are consistently funny. Zero console errors over a full multi-day session.
